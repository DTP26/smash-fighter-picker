Find your ideal Smash Ultimate character!

Put attributes you'd like your character to have on 'Preferred'.
Your result will be as close to as many of your preferred stats as possible.

Attributes that you NEED to have can be set to 'Mandatory'.
Do note that too many requirements may lead finding no results!

Leave attributes that you don't care about on 'Ignore'.
They will not be factored into the search.

Base Stats

How fast the character can move across the ground.
(Fast: greater than 1.85. Slow: less than 1.6)

How far the character dashes in short bursts.
(Fast: greater than 2.05. Slow: less than 1.82S)

How far the character will be knocked away when hit.
(Heavy: greater than 101. Light: less than 90)

How quickly the character moves forward in the air.
(Fast: greater than 1.14. Slow: less than 0.97)

How quickly the character can change directions in the air.
(Fast: greater than 0.085. Slow: less than 0.6)

How fast the character will/can fall to the ground.
(Combines fall speed and fast fall speed for simplicity.
Fast/Floaty: at least one is fast/slow, other is average
Average: They are both average/they are opposites.)

Toolkit

Whether the character can become invulnerable/mitigate damage earlier than frame 3 (standard spotdodge/airdodge frame).

Whether the character has more than 1 double jump.

Whether the character has a reflector, an absorption move, or anything that strictly beats projectiles.

Whether the character has consistent access to an item.

Whether the character has a "zair" (aerial attack triggered by grabbing midair).

Whether the character can undergo significant changes in stats/additions to moveset during the match.

Playstyle

What range you generally want to play neutral from. This is less objective than the other categories and is actively being revised. I recommend using Preferred instead of Mandatory.
