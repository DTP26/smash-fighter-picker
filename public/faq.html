Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do the matchup calculations work?
  • Matchups are ranked based on opinions from both sides of the matchup. One side's opinion is either that they win, lose, or go about even. Characters are more likely to be chosen to cover a matchup if both sides agree on a relatively positive result.

    For example, lets say I'm searching for a character that beats Fox. Assume that Fox players say they lose to both Falco and R.O.B. If Falco players say they beat Fox, but R.O.B players say that it's even, Falco will be given a higher score.
  • How do you find opinions on the matchups?
  • One of two ways. The easy way is finding a joint chart made somewhat recently with enough good players and rolling with it. The hard (and WAY more often needed) way is me manually searching for individual charts (3 per character currently) from top/high level players and aggrgeating the results myself. I try my best to search the best players from different regions to get a wider range of opinions, and I'd like to increase the number of charts over time, but I only have so much free time to burn. If you'd like to confirm the validity of the charts, they are all linked here .
  • Why don't you seperate slight losing/winning from hard /losing/winning?
  • If I eventually get every playerbase to make joint matchup charts, I would add this, but there is too much inconsistency between how diffeent players style/format their charts. Some players go from +2 to -2, some just say winning/losing, and there's simply not enough consistency in how good/bad players consider a particular ranking. This was especially apparent in Japanese matchup charts that would only go as far as +1 and utilize +0.25 at times.

    For now though, you can consider "slight winning" as one side of the matchup thinking it's winning, and the other thinking it's losing, while "hard winning" is both sides believing it's winning for one.
  • How do you categorize a character's base stats?
  • I went through each stat and determined breakpoints to create three groups amongst the roster based on the mean, median, and making the groups somewhat comparable in size. Below are all of the stat breakpoints.

    Run Speed: Below 1.6 is slow, above 1.85 is fast. Initial Dash: Below 1.825 is slow, above 2.05 is fast. Weight: Below 90 is light, above 101 is heavy. Air Speed: Below 0.97 is slow, above 1.14 is fast. Air Acceleration: below 0.6 is slow, above 0.085 is fast. Normal Fall Speed: Combination of fall speed (below 1.5 is slow, 1.7 is fast) and gravity (insert values here). Fast Fall Speed: below 2.4 is floaty, above 2.7 is fast.
  • Why did you make this in the first place?
  • This started as a small project I did for college. Originally, it had none of the matchup functionality and wasn't even a website; it was a command line program that scraped info from ultimate frame data. After I graduated, I wanted to revist the project with more time and, besides reworking it into a website, I really wanted to add the matchup functionality due to how often people tend to ask the question "who should I play to beat [insert list here]".
  • What do you plan on adding next?
  • I plan on adding a page that, given a list of characters, recommends who from that list to play against everyone in the roster.
    I will also add a page that simply shows all of the information tied to a character, both for convenience and transparency.
    I'd eventually like to add a couple more questions to the "playstyle" section of the smash picker, such as execution difficulty and camping/cheesing/"fundies", but these are much less set in stone.
